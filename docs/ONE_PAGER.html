Executive Summary

In digital lending operations governed by Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regulations, an unlogged collections call is an audit failure and a compliance violation. Yet conversational voice pipelines chaining Speech-to-Text (STT), Large Language Models (LLM), and Text-to-Speech (TTS) inherently face unpredictable latency, high concurrency, and downstream failure rates of 20% or more. Steadfast is a carrier-grade inference orchestration service built to enforce a singular guarantee: zero unaccounted calls. Every borrower interaction ends deterministically in the call log (with full telemetry and business outcome) or in the dead-letter queue (DLQ) (with structured error reasons, ready for automated replay). No call is ever silently dropped.

   [ DynamoDB Call Log ] ◄──────────────────────────┘
   (Persist-before-ack, PITR enabled)
  • Persist-Before-Ack & Idempotency: Calls are keyed by deterministic event_id. HTTP 200 is issued only after state persistence (DynamoDB on AWS, SQLite with synchronous=FULL locally). Downstream failures return HTTP 200 with recorded DLQ status; HTTP 500 is reserved exclusively for unrecordable errors so clients know to resend.
  • Full-Jitter Exponential Backoff: 3 attempts with full jitter (0.2 s base, 2.0 s cap), bounded by a 2.5 s attempt timeout and an 8.0 s call deadline. A rolling 100-attempt per-task circuit breaker trips at 50% failure, failing fast into the DLQ.
  • Target Tracking on In-Flight Calls: Scaled on InFlightCalls per task (target: 20 calls/task) via Little's Law, published using CloudWatch Embedded Metric Format (EMF) logs without PutMetricData API charges.
  • Zero-Downtime Blue/Green & Multi-AZ: Production traffic shifts over a 2-minute bake period with header-based pre-verification (X-Deploy-Test: 1) and automatic alarm rollbacks. Tasks are balanced across Availability Zones with clean draining.
  • RBI & DPDP Act Compliance: 100% data residency in Mumbai (ap-south-1), zero PII in log streams, and strict campaign calling window gating (08:00–19:00 IST) for both real-time traffic and DLQ replay workers.
